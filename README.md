# Data-Analysis-Dashboard
Today I created an Apex Retail Sales Dashboard in Excel to analyze overall business performance and identify important sales trends. The dashboard presents key metrics in a clear and interactive format, making it easier to understand retail performance at a glance.
Key areas included:
Total sales and profit
Quantity by payment
Sales by salesperson
Profit by category
Regional sales performance
Top-performing products
Key insights:
The dashboard highlights the best-performing products and categories.
Monthly trends help identify periods of high and low sales.
Regional analysis shows which locations contribute the most revenue.
Comparing sales and profit helps evaluate business performance more effectively.
This dashboard supports better decision-making by transforming raw retail data into meaningful visual insights.
